# Superstore Sales & Profit Dashboard (Power BI)

##  Overview
This project analyzes a retail Superstore dataset using Power BI to evaluate business performance across sales, profitability, and regional markets. The goal is to identify growth trends, detect loss-making areas, and provide actionable insights for better decision-making.

---

##  Key Features
- Interactive KPI tracking: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Profit Margin
- Monthly sales trend analysis to identify growth patterns
- Profitability breakdown by product sub-category
- Regional sales comparison across global markets
- Dynamic visuals enabling quick business insights

---

##  Key Insights
- Sales show a consistent upward trend over time, indicating business growth
- The **Tables** sub-category generates negative profit, highlighting pricing or cost inefficiencies
- **Copiers and Phones** are the most profitable categories, driving overall performance
- Significant variation exists across regions, suggesting opportunities for targeted expansion

---

##  Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development & visualization  
- **DAX** – Calculated measures (Total Sales, Profit, Profit Margin)  
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation  

---

##  Business Impact
This dashboard helps stakeholders:
- Monitor key performance indicators in real time  
- Identify underperforming products and optimize pricing strategies  
- Focus on high-profit categories to maximize revenue  
- Analyze regional trends to guide market expansion decisions
